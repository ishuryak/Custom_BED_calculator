## 5. Full-text check of six downloaded papers (added 2026-10-18)

Six papers were read in full: Fowler 2003, Stuschke 1999, Fenwick 2008, Hartley 2011, HYPNO 2026 and HYHOPE 2025. Copies are in `Custom_BED_calculator_v2/References/` (excluded from git). Each published value below was recomputed with the app's formulas.

### Fowler et al. 2003 (the early-toxicity model)

- **Parameters confirmed:** α 0.35 Gy⁻¹ (text: "0.3–0.35"), α/β 10 Gy, Tk 7 d, Tp 2.5 d. Tk between 5 and 10 days made no difference to the ranking of schedules.
- **Time counting confirmed as elapsed days:**
  - Table 1 lists "70 Gy/7 w = 46 d" with BED 53.1. The app's formula with T = 46 gives 53.1.
  - CAIR, 35 × 2 Gy on 7 days/week, is "34 d" with BED 62.6. The formula gives 62.6.

  This settles decision D1.
- **What the zone is:** an empirical gap between schedules that clinicians reported as intolerable (discontinued, "above MTD") and those reported as tolerable.
  - The schedules come from Tables 1 and 2: 29 rows, some repeated.
  - The first estimate, 59–63 Gy₁₀, was narrowed to a "hazy edge" of 59–61. The discussion also mentions 59–62.
  - It is a rule of thumb, not a probability.
  - Notably, the standard 70 Gy/35 fractions (53.1) is labeled "Near tol?" in Table 1.
- **Directly relevant to the feasibility trial:** "if the nominal BED approaches the zone of 59–61 Gy₁₀, then a preliminary dose-escalation clinical trial should be considered to observe acute reactions." This should be quoted on the page.
- **Chemotherapy:** weekly 30–40 mg/m² or 3-weekly 75–100 mg/m² cisplatin "may … add approximately 3–5 points (Gy₁₀) to the radiation-alone BED levels, equivalent to two or three 2-Gy fractions". This is described as a provisional estimate from the authors' own observations. The app's lowered zone (54–58) is equivalent to that statement. Adding 3–5 Gy₁₀ to the acute BED instead would be an alternative way to show it.
- **Stated limitations:** no treatment volume or mucosal area; pre-IMRT data; complete repair assumed; not applicable to other mucosal sites. Add these to the app's limitations.

### Fenwick et al. 2008

- **Time counting:** the paper states it explicitly. T is counted "including the first and last days of treatment. The Tk value of 8 days is 1 day longer than the value used by Fowler et al., reflecting a difference in the schedule duration definition." This independently confirms D1.
- **Its own tolerance boundary:** fitted on 84 schedules (57 regular ones).
  - Boundary: cell-kill BED ≤ 69.5·(T/32.2)/sin(T/32.2) − 3.5 Gy₁₀, with T inclusive and ≤ 50 days, including incomplete repair. "All schedules analyzed … below [it] are tolerable."
  - The boundary curves, so it is not a fixed line.
  - Recomputed for once-daily schedules:

    | 35 × 2 Gy | 84.0 vs 98.6 | below |
    | HYPNO 55/20 | 70.1 vs 74.2 | below |
    | 20 × 2.9 Gy | 74.8 vs 74.2 | above |
    | HYHOPE 46.5/15 | 60.9 vs 70.2 | below |

- **Candidate new option (D11):** use this as an alternative early-toxicity limit. It is better founded than the fixed 59 Gy₁₀ line and needs no separate repopulation parameters.

### Hartley et al. 2011

- **Parameters:**
  - Acute mucosa: α 0.3 Gy⁻¹, Tk 7, Tp 2.5, with T = 46 days for 35 fractions (elapsed). Recomputed 70/35/46 at α 0.3: 48.0, matching their table.
  - Tumor: α 0.3, Tk 22 d, Tp 3 d, which is 0.77 Gy/day from day 22. This is a possible literature preset (D12).
- **Validation strength:**
  - The link between acute BED and acute grade 3 mucositis across randomized trials was only a trend (p = 0.06).
  - Tumor BED vs local control was strong (p = 0.006).
  - The "15% decrease in late mucosal BED" statement is confirmed (Fig. 5).

### Stuschke & Thames 1999

- **Tumor α/β:** 10.5 Gy, given as 6.5–29 in the abstract and 6.5–27.0 in the results.
- **What it is based on:** hyperfractionation trials with 1.1–2.55 Gy per fraction. Using it for 2.5–3+ Gy fractions is an extrapolation.
- **Late α/β:** from a single trial, EORTC 22791. It is 3.3–4.0 Gy (confidence limits 2.2–6.7) for grade 2+ fibrosis and other late effects, depending on the assumed dose-response slope. The page currently quotes only "4.0 (3.3–5.0)" and should give the range.

### HYHOPE 2025 (outcomes)

- **Population:** favorable-risk HPV-positive oropharynx (T1-3 N0-2, ≤ 10 pack-years), elective neck reduced, 3 × weekly cisplatin 40 mg/m².
- **Dose:** 46.5 Gy/15 fractions was the maximum tolerated dose. It was designed as BED-equivalent to 60 Gy in 30 fractions, a de-escalation, not equivalent to 70 Gy.
- **Treatment time:** median 18 days (range 18–22). This is an elapsed-day count, while the protocol table used 19.
- **Toxicity:** grade 3 oral mucositis 21% (5/24), any acute grade 3 29%, temporary feeding tube 8%, no late grade 3+. Dose-limiting toxicity was defined as grade 4, or persistent grade 3 mucositis at 3 months.
- **Control:** 2-year locoregional recurrence 13.8% (3.2–32%).
- **App, Fowler convention:** acute BED 52.2.
- The paper also confirms Jacinto 2018's nasogastric-tube rate (75%) and the carboplatin series (84% grade 3 mucositis; that is Madhava 2006).

### What the five papers add

- **Fowler 2010 (BJR 83:554).**
  - Table 4, tumor: "Tk = 21–32 days and Tp = 3 days for lung or head and neck cancer", α = 0.35 (occasionally 0.3) Gy⁻¹. With α 0.35 this is 0.66 Gy₁₀ per day.
  - Text: multifraction radiotherapy is advantageous "provided that ... tumour repopulation averages no more than about 0.7 Gy/day".
  - Tumor onset: published human determinations range 18–32 days, but "repopulation is detectable in animal tumours within a few days after irradiation, which suggests earlier rather than later".
  - Mucosal onset: labelled (proliferating) cells in human mucosal biopsies "fell rapidly to about 10% at seven days after starting irradiation with 2-Gy fractions, and then began to rise sharply", which fixed the mucosal Tk at 7 days (Fowler's ref 45, not read by me).
  - Fowler's 2008 "practical overall time" method is the same idea as the app's search: maximize tumor BED under a late limit (117 Gy₃) and an acute limit (mid grey zone, 61 Gy₁₀).
- **Meade 2014 (Tumour Biol 35:2017).**
  - Writes the Fowler ceiling as 61 + 0.792·(T − 7) and evaluates 70 Gy/35 fractions at T = 46 days (91.9 Gy₁₀). This is a third source for the elapsed-day convention (D1).
  - Chemotherapy contribution to acute mucosal BED: global 5.1 Gy₁₀; single-agent cisplatin 300 mg/m² gives 4.4 Gy₁₀, scaled down in proportion to the dose actually given (2.4–4.4 Gy₁₀ across trials). At this scaling, HYPNO's cumulative 121 mg/m² corresponds to about 1.8 Gy₁₀ (my extrapolation, not in the paper).
  - Its Fenwick formula uses T_c = 50 days, a rounding of Fenwick's own 32.2-day constant (2·50/π = 31.8). The app, if D11 is approved, should use Fenwick's 32.2.
  - Advice: "a regime predicted to be intolerable by at least two of the three models should undergo careful phase 1 testing". Volume is not in any model.
- **Madhava 2006 (Clin Oncol 18:77).** 55 Gy/20 fractions with carboplatin (AUC 3.5–5, days 1 and 21): grade 3 mucositis in 16 of 19 (84%), lasting 4 weeks or more in 3. Confirms the source of HYHOPE's 84% figure. Recommended phase 2 dose AUC 4.5.
- **Miller 2026 (Clin Oncol 59:104332).** Real-world 55 Gy/20 fractions, 2 UK centers, VMAT, no concurrent chemotherapy (one exception), older and less fit patients (mean age 72, 37% performance status ≥ 2). Of 113 completing treatment: grade 3 mucositis (RTOG) at end of treatment 64% (72), falling to 26% at 4 weeks; grade 3 dysphagia 39%; gastrostomy 45% (87% prophylactic); 12-month tube dependence 15%. 95% completed treatment. This is higher than HYPNO's 50.9%, retrospective, and graded at a single time point.
- **Kirkpatrick 2010 (IJROBP 76:S42).** Spinal cord α/β = 0.87 Gy (0.54–1.19). Full cord cross-section in 2-Gy fractions: 50, 60 and 69 Gy give 0.2%, 6% and 50% myelopathy. Needed only for the organ-at-risk panel. It also shows why one late α/β of 3 Gy cannot stand in for the cord.

### The DD parameters: what the defaults imply

Defaults: α = 0.2 Gy⁻¹, α/β = 10 Gy, λ_DI = 0.2 per day, T_k = 28 days, λ_DD = 0.5 per day. The "maximum repopulation rate" of the DD model is λ_DD = 0.5 (γ in the 2018 paper, Eq. 4); 0.2 per day is the DI rate.

| Quantity at the 35 × 2 Gy reference | DI | DD (λ_DD 0.5) |
|---|---|---|
| Repopulation onset | 28 d | 21.7 d |
| Repopulation rate | 0.200 /d (Tp 3.5 d) | 0.150 /d (Tp 4.6 d) |
| BED lost per day | 1.00 Gy₁₀ | 0.75 Gy₁₀ |

- **The DI rate itself is not high.** Tp = 3.5 days is close to Fowler's 3 days. What is high is the BED loss per day, λ/α = 1.0 Gy₁₀, because α = 0.2 is low. The literature values are 0.72 (Withers), 0.66 (Fowler 2010, α 0.35, Tp 3), 0.77 (Hartley, α 0.3, Tp 3) and 0.65 (HYPNO design). The calculator's 1.0 is above all of them.
- **This matters for the suggestions.** With the app's search defaults (gain ≥ 1 Gy, ≥ 7 days saved, early BED ≤ standard), the calculator defaults give 7 regimens. The Withers, Fowler 2010 and Hartley sets give 0, because the strict early limit then binds before any tumor gain appears. With the Fowler 59 Gy₁₀ cap they give 12–19 regimens. All are credited by DI only.
- **HYPNO as a check (test minus control, Gy₁₀).** DI: +0.9 (calculator), −1.9 (Withers), −1.2 (Fowler 2010), +0.2 (Hartley). DD with λ_DD 0.5: about −4.7 to −5.0 under every set. The trial found 3-year locoregional control within 1.4 percentage points between arms and passed its 10% noninferiority margin. That is closer to the DI prediction. It is not conclusive against DD: 75% received cisplatin, and a 10% margin cannot exclude a deficit of a few Gy.
- **λ_DD cannot be lowered much.** Calibration requires DD to reproduce DI's loss at the reference. With the defaults that needs λ_DD ≥ 0.27; below that the DD model becomes unavailable. Lowering λ_DD from 0.5 toward 0.3 moves the reference onset earlier (21.7 → 4.9 days) and makes DD harsher on short courses. Raising it makes DD kinder; at λ_DD ≈ 2, DD credits 15 regimens.
- **A principled value: match DD to DI at the reference.** Setting λ_DD = λ_DI / P_kill,ref (0.665 at the defaults) makes the two models agree at the 35 × 2 Gy reference in BED, onset (28 d) and rate (0.2/d). They then differ only where daily and cumulative cell killing differ from the reference. This removes λ_DD as a free choice. Among the regimens the search returns, the realized DD rate is then 0.21–0.27 per day (Tp 2.6–3.3 days), inside the 2–5 day range reported clinically. Effect: DD BEDs of the suggested regimens rise by 0.4–2.4 Gy₁₀ and stay 1.1–7.7 Gy₁₀ below the reference; no list changes. Under the literature presets the matched value is close to 0.5 anyway (0.48–0.56).

### A minimum DD onset of 7 days: plausibility and effect

- **Effect on results.** Tested with floors of 0 and 7 days, λ_DD 0.5, matched and 2, criteria "either", "DD only" and "DD only with the Fowler cap": no suggested regimen changes in any combination. All suggested regimens already have DD onsets of 15–27 days. The floor changes only very short courses entered by hand: 5 × 8 Gy goes from 66.6 to 72.0 Gy₁₀ and 10 × 5 Gy from 64.7 to 66.1 Gy₁₀. These fail the toxicity limits anyway.
- **For plausibility.**
  - Accelerated repopulation is a response to cell depletion. Lethally damaged cells usually die only after one or more divisions, and dead cells take time to clear, so a lag between the start of treatment and the proliferative response is expected.
  - In human oral mucosa that lag is about 7 days with 2-Gy fractions (Fowler 2010, citing biopsy cell counts).
  - The unfloored DD model can place onset at 3–5 days for intense short courses. That is faster than anything observed clinically for head and neck tumors (18–32 days, Fowler 2010; 4 ± 1 weeks, Withers 1988).
- **Against, or not established.**
  - No human tumor data set a minimum. Fowler 2010 notes repopulation in animal tumors "within a few days after irradiation".
  - Mucosa and tumor are different tissues, so the 7-day mucosal value is an analogy, not evidence for tumors.
  - The 7-day mucosal onset was measured with 2-Gy fractions. Whether it is shorter with larger daily doses is exactly the question the DD model asks, and the floor would assume the answer.
- **Direction of the error.** A floor removes repopulation, so it raises tumor BED for short courses. For a trial-design tool this is the optimistic direction for the experimental arm.
- **Verdict.** A minimum lag is biologically reasonable. Seven days is a defensible order of magnitude, but it is an assumption, not a measured tumor value. Recommended as an adjustable input with a stated rationale, not as a hidden constant.
- **Implementation if approved.** Onset = max(T_min, C·T/(αK)), with a hard max. If the reference onset itself falls below T_min, the calibration has no solution with the floor and the DD model is marked unavailable, as now. One unit test reproduces the 5 × 8 Gy value with and without the floor and is checked to fail on the unfloored code.

### Can α and λ_DD together make the DD model plausible for all clinical regimens? (2026-10-18)

- **Test.** Grid over α 0.05–0.80 Gy⁻¹ and λ_DD 0.1–30 per day, on the 13 regimens of `DI_DD_clinical_regimens_2026_10_18.docx`. DI daily loss either fixed through λ_DI = 0.2 (loss 0.2/α) or set to 0.72 (Withers) or 0.66 (Fowler) Gy₁₀/day. A regimen passes if, whenever its DD onset falls before its last day, the onset, doubling time and daily loss are all in band. Loose bands: onset 18–35 d, doubling 2–6 d, loss 0.5–1.0 Gy₁₀/day. Strict bands: 21–35 d, 3–5 d, 0.6–0.8.
- **Result.** No combination passes all 13 in any of the six variant-band pairs. Best: 10 of 13 (loose bands, α ≈ 0.31, λ_DD ≈ 0.52, reference onset day 30), failing HYHOPE, GORTEC and CHART. Strict bands: at best 7–8 of 13.
- **Why.** The DD onset of any regimen is onset_ref × (T/K)/(T/K)_ref. This ratio depends only on the schedule (α/β fixed), not on α or λ_DD: HYPNO 0.66, TROG 0.61, HYHOPE 0.56, GORTEC 0.52, CHART 0.35. Keeping the short courses from repopulating before about day 18 (or before they end) needs a reference onset of about 35 days, the top of the clinical range. The calibration then squeezes the DI-equivalent loss (19 days of DI repopulation) into about 12 days, so repopulation at the reference must be about 1.6 times faster than DI's (doubling near 2.2 days with λ_DI = 0.2). Regimens with more daily cell kill repopulate faster still. α only trades doubling time against Gy₁₀/day (loss = rate/α); it cannot fix both, and it moves the DI loss too.
- **Implication.** The implausibility sits in the DD onset rule (onset proportional to T/K) combined with the calibration to DI at the reference, not in the parameter values. Options to fix it would change the model: (i) a weaker onset dependence, e.g. onset ∝ (T/K)^k with k < 1; (ii) rate-only dose dependence, with the onset fixed as in DI; (iii) dropping exact calibration to DI at the reference. Each needs a decision and, ideally, a fit to trial data, as in the 2018 analysis.
