## 4. Findings to carry forward

- **The DD onset ratio between two regimens is fixed by their schedules** (T/K), whatever α and λ_DD are. With the calibration to DI at the reference, no parameter choice makes onset, rate and daily loss all plausible for short courses.
- **Trial direction.** At every plausible setting the DD model predicts a tumor BED loss for the GORTEC and TROG accelerated arms (observed gains of 18.2 and 8.5 points) and a deficit of about 6 Gy₁₀ for HYPNO, which was noninferior with a locoregional control difference of 1.4 points or less. The DI model matches the direction of 8 of 9 trial comparisons at the current defaults.
- **The default DI daily loss of 1.0 Gy₁₀ (α 0.2, λ_DI 0.2)** is above all published estimates (0.65–0.77). With any published set, no regimen passes the strict "early BED no higher than standard" limit.
- **Real-world and trial toxicity for 55 Gy/20 fractions:**
  - HYPNO grade 3+ mucositis 50.9% (75% received cisplatin)
  - Miller 2026: 64% at the end of treatment, without chemotherapy
  - Madhava 2006 with carboplatin: 84%.
- **Chemotherapy contribution to acute mucosal BED:** about 4.4 Gy₁₀ per 300 mg/m² cisplatin, scaled to the dose given (Meade 2014).
