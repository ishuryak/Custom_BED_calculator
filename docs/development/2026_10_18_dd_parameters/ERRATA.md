# Errata for the 2026-10-18 DD parameter notes

The two notes in this folder (`DI_DD_clinical_regimens_2026_10_18`, `DD_parameter_grid_search_2026_10_18`) and their scripts are kept unchanged as records of commit `6f0209c`. Their scripts source the app's `R/bed_models.R` from a Windows temporary folder and use literal calibration values, so they are not rerunnable as they stand, and version 2.1 functions now take a calibration anchor and convert the acute clock themselves. The statements below are wrong or too strong. Sources: Review B (Codex, second review) and Review A, both summarized in `../2026_10_18_synthesis_plan.md`.

| Where | Statement | Correction |
|---|---|---|
| Both notes, benchmarks | HYPNO design "0.65 Gy per day" listed with the Gy₁₀ losses | HYPNO's 0.65 Gy/day is in EQD2 units (66 Gy + 9 d × 0.65 = 71.85 Gy, HYPNO p. 389), which is 0.78 Gy₁₀/day. The quoted literature range 0.65–0.77 Gy₁₀/day mixes units. Fowler's α 0.35 with Tp 3 d gives 0.66 Gy₁₀/day, but his "about 0.7 Gy/day" in EQD2 terms is 0.84 Gy₁₀/day. |
| Both notes, limitations | Ignoring incomplete repair between same-day fractions "overstates" BED | It **understates** the cell-kill BED (the repair cross-term is nonnegative). The net effect on DD BED depends on the onset and rate, which also change. |
| Clinical regimens note, Tables 2 and 4 | "Which model matches the direction" and "closer" columns, and "DI matches 8 of 9" | A ΔBED in Gy cannot be judged closer to a difference in percentage points without a fitted dose-response relation. Only sign agreement is a diagnostic, and it does not select a model. |
| Clinical regimens note, EORTC 22791 row | Observed difference +1.0 point | Reproduces the 2018 compilation, but Stuschke 1999 reports about 38% vs 56% 5-year local control and Hartley 2011 about a 19-point difference. Exclude the row from any outcome comparison until the primary Horiot report is checked. |
| Grid note, Result 2 | Strict limits admit "only degenerate solutions" | No combination passes all 13 regimens under strict limits (the maximum is 10 of 13). Degenerate high-λ_DD points exist but do not solve the stated problem. |
| Grid note, Result 2 | "109–110 combinations" | 110 combinations meet the rate and loss limits for all 13 regimens. 109 remain if the DI daily loss must also lie in the loose band. |
| Grid note, method | A regimen whose DD onset falls after its last day "has no repopulation" | With the smooth threshold the loss is finite (for example α 0.8, λ_DD 30/day: onset 47.14 d at the 47-day reference, yet a 4.75 Gy₁₀ loss). Report integrated loss, nominal onset and rate separately. |
| Grid note, implications, and handoff section 4 | The direction mismatch for accelerated arms is "structural" and needs a change to the DD model | Too strong. The fitted 2018 DD parameters (α 0.224, γ 1.17/day, C 14.5, g 0.0154) give gains for GORTEC (+3.2 Gy₁₀) and TROG (+2.1 Gy₁₀) and about no change for HYPNO (+0.5). The mismatch belongs to the app's calibration (shared α, DD = DI at the anchor) together with plausibility bands taken from DI-model studies. The bands exclude the fitted region (late onset, fast rate). |
| Both notes, early BED columns | Acute BED from the app's `compute_all()` on the inclusive clock | 0.79 Gy₁₀ too low for comparison with Fowler's zone (version 2.1 converts to elapsed days). Columns computed with `bed_early(T − 1)` were already correct. |
| Plan `PLAN_BED_calculator_v2_2026_10_18.md` (outside the repo), OAR inverse | Formula with f² inside the root | For B = n f d (1 + f d/r): d = r/(2f) · [√(1 + 4B/(n r)) − 1]. Example: f 0.8, r 3, B 85.8667, n 20 gives d = 2.987 Gy. |
